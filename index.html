What is Alpha?

A conversation, not a lecture.

Alpha runs in cities, campuses, and homes all over the world, and now it's coming to campus, hosted at Saint Mary's University and open to students from any school. Each session starts with a meal, moves into a short talk on a big question (Is there a God? Why does the world have so much suffering? What's the point of life?), and ends in small-group conversation where every question is welcome and nobody's put on the spot.

You don't need to know anything about faith, believe anything in particular, or come back every week. Come as you are, bring a friend, and see where the conversation goes.

"No pressure, no follow-up, no charge, just an honest space to ask what you've always wondered about."
